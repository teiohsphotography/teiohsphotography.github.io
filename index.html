About Me

"Photography is pretty simple stuff. You just react to what you see, and take many, many pictures."

Hi, my name is Teioh and I'm a photographer based out of Ventura County and Los Angeles. I take all types of portrait photos ranging from animals and graduation shoots to intricate cosplay setups and candid human moments.

My journey started with animal photography, mainly taking photos of my own and my neighbors' pets. If you've ever tried to negotiate a professional sit-down portrait with a hyperactive golden retriever or a cat that actively resents your existence, you know that normal human portraiture is a walk in the park by comparison. That trial by fire taught me two invaluable life skills: lightning-fast shutter reflexes and supreme patience.

Turns out, humans aren't all that different from pets. Most people get a little tense in front of a giant lens until someone makes them laugh or hands them a snack. Whether we're celebrating years of hard work for your graduation, capturing an elaborate handcrafted cosplay build, or braving the Ventura Freeway gridlock just in time to catch that iconic Southern California sunset, my goal is pretty straightforward: make the entire session fun, keep it stress-free, and ensure you walk away looking effortlessly cool.

FAQs

How do I book a session?

Send me an email through the Connect page with the type of shoot you're after, your preferred date, and the location you have in mind. Graduation season fills up quickly, so reach out early if you're planning a grad shoot.

Where do you shoot?

I'm based in Ventura County and Los Angeles and shoot throughout both. If you have a location in mind farther out, just ask.

Most of my graduation shoots are at USC, UCLA, and Moorpark College, but I'm happy to shoot at other locations too.

What should I wear?

Wear something you feel comfortable and confident in. Solid colors usually photograph better than busy patterns or large logos, and bringing a second outfit is always welcome.

Why do you charge what you charge?

Fair question! On paper it's an hour and a half of me running around with a camera, occasionally lying in the grass for the right angle. What you don't see is everything around it: the drive there and back (anyone who's sat on the 101 knows that can be its own adventure), and most importantly, the editing.

After every session I go through hundreds of shots to find the best ones, then edit each one by hand: color, lighting, skin, stray hairs, and the occasional stranger who wandered into the background. That part alone can take several hours, and it's the part I care about most, because I want you to end up with photos you genuinely love and can't wait to share. So you're not just paying for the 1.5 hours; you're paying for the hours after, when I'm hunched over my laptop making sure every photo does you justice.
